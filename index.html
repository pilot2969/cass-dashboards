How to read this board

We do NOT track every variable. We track only transitions that clear a relevance threshold. Every number is a bet on a mechanism, and every bet is backed by a visible evidence base and an armed falsifier. A forecast that cannot be falsified is not a forecast — it is a mood. When a forecast stops clearing the bar, it moves to the accountability record below.

📖 Methodology — how this board is built and how it is tested ▶
The MGET framework. The board runs on a four-leg model of systemic failure: Leg 1 — complexity cap / material substrate (energy, logistics, money); Leg 2 — elite overproduction / class friction; Leg 3 — enclosure mutation (institutions becoming opaque, weaponized, or unaccountable); Leg 4 — vanguard alignment (coordinated counter-elites forming). Each leg is scored 0–15 from visible, public evidence only.
Forecasts are bets on mechanisms, not dates. Each entry names its mechanism, its evidence base, and its falsifier — the specific condition that would retire it. When a falsifier triggers, the forecast is retired publicly, on this page. A forecast that cannot be falsified is not a forecast; it is a mood.
Lead-time measurement. Every forecast shows the date it was logged and the date it resolved. The gap between the two is our lead time — measured, not claimed.
The Threshold Watch. The watch is the board's public storm signal. A watch does not say an event is happening; it says conditions are primed. It carries an expiry date, is re-checked weekly, and can only do three things: upgrade, hold, or expire. It never quietly lapses.
Blind backtesting. The model is validated by reconstructing past eras using only period-available information, then scoring the call against what actually happened. The protocol requires three passes: correct mechanism class, directional macro call, and nulls produced where nulls were warranted. To date, twelve eras and one counter-case battery have been tested:
• Nov 2019 (pre-corridor): board printed 22%, no watch — correct null.
• Sept 2001 (exogenous shock): macro silent at 12%; the vanguard leg caught the shock class at 35–40%.
• Sept 2008 (corridor detonation): board printed ~70%, watch issued; detonation landed inside the stated 30-day window.
• Full historical sweep (n=8): Reformation 1517, Glorious Revolution 1688, French Revolution 1787, 1848 revolutions, 1968 counterculture, plus three control eras — legs scored on period wire only. Score: 6 of 8 clean PASS, 1 PARTIAL, 1 MISSED.
• Counter-case battery: Soviet 1965, Singapore 1990, China 2000 — raw opacity shows ~zero conversion to decay timing; Leg 3's mutation signal, not opacity itself, carries the timing power.
Full protocol: CASS Blind Backtest Protocol (Standing Methodology), Samuel Adams, 2026. Sample size: n=3 original + n=8 historical sweep + 3 counter-cases (12 eras total). Known limits are stated in the protocol, including the risk of outcome contamination in self-graded runs and the provenance caveat that sweep eras are reconstructions rather than true blind runs. Per-era leg scores and grades are recorded in the MGET control ledger.
📋 Data Dictionary — every field on this board, defined ▶

Definitions for every term used in the Forecast Register, Macro Forecast, and MGET Ledger below. Written so a first-time reader can interpret any card without prior context.

Confidence %The board's probability estimate that the statement resolves as written. Rendered on the card's badge; the sparkline beside it shows how that number has moved over time. A flat line at a fixed value means no new evidence moved the estimate — itself information.
Status: pendingStatement is live; no falsifier has triggered and no resolution date has arrived.
Status: emergentPartial confirmations are accumulating but the statement's full resolution criteria are not yet met — the mechanism is visibly underway.
Status: confirmedThe falsifier's symmetric criteria have been met — the event happened as specified, on record.
Status: failedThe falsifier triggered against the prediction. Logged publicly — misses stay on the board.
FalsifierThe specific, dated, observable condition that would kill or resolve the prediction. Written before the outcome is known. If the falsifier is ambiguous, the prediction is not instrument-grade.
Evidence confirmedFacts on the record that support the statement. Listed itemized so each can be independently checked.
Evidence contradictedFacts on the record that cut against the statement. Logged with equal prominence — the board's honesty test.
Watch (forward)Named signals to monitor next — the specific events that would move confidence up or down.
⏱ lead timeInterval from the log date to the resolution date, shown on resolved entries. The board's calibration record.
Macro ForecastThe board's single highest-conviction systemic call. One at a time; falsifiers armed; retired publicly if triggered.
MGET LedgerRunning log of mechanism tests: each entry records a real-world event scored against the model's legs (complexity cap, elite overproduction, enclosure mutation, vanguard alignment). Verdicts: confirms / strains / undermines / neutral.
MGET legsThe four structural legs of the model being tested by each ledger entry. An entry may test one or several.
Forward signalWhat the ledger entry implies to watch next — feeding the next day's scan.
SparklinePer-card confidence trend line from history.json — daily snapshots, oldest to newest, red if rising, green if falling. Hover for dated values.
Watch (term)A watch is NOT a prediction. It means conditions are primed — if a spark lands, it will travel fast.
