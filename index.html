How to read this board

We do NOT track every variable. We track only transitions that clear a relevance threshold. Every number is a bet on a mechanism, and every bet is backed by a visible evidence base and an armed falsifier. A forecast that cannot be falsified is not a forecast — it is a mood. When a forecast stops clearing the bar, it moves to the accountability record below.
