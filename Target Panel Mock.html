Section 3.2 — Aim, not flank

MOCK · proposed replacement for the flank field · pilot on 24 randomly drawn specimens of 224 · payload inlined, renders from disk

Why this is the better field

Flank asks who produced this. That is not in the text — it is inferred from the producer, and the blind recode showed the inference reproduces at κ 0.53. Aim asks who is the degraded object here. That is in the text: the slur, the category claim, the group named in the caption. Two coders reading the same post can agree on it without knowing who wrote it.

It also stops the corpus depending on a feed you cannot audit for the half of the finding that matters least. You already accept the direction is right-dominant; the open questions are which populations are being constructed as subhuman, and whether the aim set is broadening. Flank cannot answer either.

Proposed aim taxonomy

LGBT / trans people — existence denial, bestialization, predation frame, "mentally ill" construction.
Muslims — category claims about Islam, civilizational incompatibility, blanket threat attribution.
Immigrants / foreigners — invasiveness, contamination, "not our people," removal as cleansing.
Democrats, left, liberals — "leftist," "progressive," named Democratic officials where the frame is the party.
Press, pollsters, experts — the epistemic class: "fake polls," "phony pollster," journalists as enemies, credentialed people as liars.
Republicans / own side — RINO, traitor, insufficiently loyal, "not a real conservative." New cell, and it matters: it separates party-dehumanization from movement-dehumanization.
Women · Black people — separate codes; do not fold into "Democrats."
Institutions / the state — courts, agencies, Congress as a body. Sacralization specimens land here.
Foreign states — Iran, Houthis, Venezuela. Adversary framing, not population framing.
No human target — technical reporting, market data, control baselines, pure strategic analysis.

Coding rules: code the object, not the speaker. A post by a right-flank account about RINOs aims at Republicans. Multi-aim allowed and expected — record every target with a stated aim, cap at four. Where the object is a named individual, code the group the individual stands for and say so in the record. Where aim is genuinely absent, code none — do not force a flank.

Any automated pass on this field produces a wrong finding. The gap is not noise; it is the speaker leaking into the target. Aim has to be coded.

What this does not fix

  • The sampler. Aim counts how the corpus is composed, not how the world is. Encounter-rule capture still passes through your ranked feed. Aim inherits that defect untouched.
  • Intensity. Aim says who, not how far down the ladder. Pair it with the existing bestialization / existence-denial / extermination scale and you get aim × severity, which is the shape you actually want on the wall.
  • Single-name specimens. A post aimed at one official still needs the group code, and that is a judgment call — the likeliest place two coders diverge. Reliability testing goes there first.
