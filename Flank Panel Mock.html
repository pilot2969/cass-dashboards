    E · The sampler problem no relabelling touches

    The capture rule is an encounter rule — "whenever SA encounters a dehumanizing post in the feed, capture it." That samples a ranked stream, not a population. Correcting the flank field changes the labels; it cannot change what reached the feed.

    • Don't print 92%. Print direction, and the corrected share only with the caveat riding next to it.
    • Re-label the structure stratum (39 → ~79) so the denominator is specimens, not specimens plus filings.
    • Fixed-scroll census — first N posts, code everything, skip nothing — is the only version that yields a share defensible in public.

    Known limit: the blind pass coded scrubbed ~300-character gists; median record is 1,867. Six disagreements are structural-power specimens that may resolve back to their stored label on a full read — sacralization-legislative-halls, ice-voter-records-dragnet-thread, rsbn-website-architecture among them. 84% is provisional until a full-text round.
