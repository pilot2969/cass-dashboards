7 · Method, provenance, and what is broken

Definitions in force

channel national = pundit/outlet supply watch · feed = algorithmic encounter · both = feed capture carrying outlet content · direct = named account or page captured one at a time · desk = wire/policy reporting with no post artefact.
aim Code the object, not the speaker. A right-flank post about RINOs aims at Republicans. Multi-aim allowed, cap four. Where aim is genuinely absent, none.
reaction none / complained / defected / unknown — audience-side response. Defined, populated nowhere.

Three things a reviewer should not trust

1. The feed channel records hits only. Penetration is not measurable from this corpus, because misses are never recorded. A supply list of 8–12 narratives and their presence-or-absence in the feed is the missing instrument.
2. Aim is 1 coded. Every aim figure except that one record's is pilot output and is marked as such. Do not print a target share until the field is fully coded.
3. Channel was backfilled by keyword. 15 hand-checked, roughly 2 mismatches — 85–90% accurate, provisional, and reclassified on sight during normal work.
What does hold: totals, dates, theme, and flank labels. Those come from the records as written and are not derived.
