Up to 250,000 ballots affected across 332 polling sites in more than 40 municipalities, diverted to emergency ballot bags for central tabulation. This is **NY-17, Lawler against Conley** — a Harris-won district sitting inside the House majority arithmetic. The frame's first concrete specimen landed in a seat that can decide control.

Now the part the frame has to survive, and it is not surviving it yet:

- Both candidates urged voters to stay in line and vote, and both said every ballot would be counted. Lawler: "Do not leave the polling place without voting."

That is election **administration** — specific, jurisdictional, disposition-oriented. It is the operational opposite of the declarative frame.
