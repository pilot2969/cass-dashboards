Definitions
Forecast / prediction
A dated claim about a future event with a stated confidence — the probability we assign that it happens.
Confidence
Our probability the claim is right. 90% means: if we made 100 such calls, we expect ~90 correct.
Big Question
A hand-picked forecast the editors flag as the trending theme of the moment.
Threshold Watch
An alert that conditions are primed — not a prediction that anything will happen.
Tested claim
A forecast put before an independent review panel; verdicts are upheld, revised, or overturned.
Register
The full list of tracked forecasts, grouped by theme.
